---
name: Portfolio editorial voice
description: How research uncertainty should inform the portfolio without making it read like an external audit.
---

Present the portfolio as the candidate explaining concrete questions and work,
not as a reviewer evaluating someone else's evidence.

**Why:** The user wants a persuasive hiring story. Repeating “not independently
verified” or “reviewed in source” throughout the public page is accurate to an
audit process but distracts from that purpose and reads as third-party criticism.

**How to apply:** Keep research provenance and unresolved questions in the
research notes. Public case studies should explain the problem, approach,
artifact and necessary scope limitations plainly. Retain explicit fictional-data,
prototype and non-live-demo labels; do not turn confident wording into invented
outcomes or authorship claims.
